Why sort a shopping list by aisle?

Because a list in shop order makes the shop quicker and easier. Write a normal list and it comes out grouped the way a supermarket is laid out: fruit and veg, bakery, dairy and eggs, meat and fish, chilled, vegetarian and vegan, frozen, cupboard, herbs and spices, world foods, snacks, drinks, household, toiletries, baby and pets. Mark anything organic with one switch. One pass round the shop instead of doubling back. Works on any phone or laptop, nothing to install. Press Share link and whoever you send the link to sees and edits the same list, live – handy for couples and housemates.

List by Aisle is free and needs no account. Your list stays in this browser on this device until you clear it – nothing is sent anywhere unless you press Copy, WhatsApp / Messenger or Email.
